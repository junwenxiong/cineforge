CineForge · 影铸 — Showcase

Every clip was made with open video models on rented Colab GPUs using the scripts in this repository. Press play for sound.

Original films (text → film)

Three original films, about 30 minutes each. No camera, no actors. Measured time and cost for the whole film are listed under each clip.

Cat recast (video-to-video)

Real TV footage → cats, same motion & original dialogue (v2v). Each shot keeps the source's depth and timing; a cat version of its first frame (plus a guide frame wherever a new face appears) sets the look; hands are masked so they come out as paws.

Music in the original-film clips: Scott Buckley, CC BY 4.0 (scottbuckley.com.au). Cat recasts are fan re-imaginings of scenes from the CCTV TV series 三国演义 (1994), 西游记 (1986) and 红楼梦 (1987); dialogue audio is from those series. GPU times exclude model loading (about 1–4 minutes per fresh Colab computer).
